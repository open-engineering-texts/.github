# Open Engineering Texts

Structured engineering knowledge for humans and machines.

![Open Engineering Texts hero-banner.png](../assets/hero-banner.png)

Open Engineering Texts is the knowledge layer of the Open Engineering ecosystem.

We create, curate, structure, and transform engineering knowledge into reusable texts — from technical explanations and reference material to investigations, guides, specifications, courses, and machine-readable engineering knowledge.

📚 What is Open Engineering Texts?

Engineering knowledge is everywhere, but it is often locked inside documents, repositories, wikis, tickets, presentations, and people’s heads.

Open Engineering Texts provides a home for engineering knowledge that can be:

* 📖 Read by engineers and learners
* 🤖 Understood by AI assistants
* 🔍 Investigated by Detective OS
* 🧩 Composed into larger engineering products
* 🎓 Transformed into courses and learning experiences
* 🗺️ Connected to the Open Engineering Map
* ♻️ Reused across projects and organizations
* 🧠 Enriched continuously as the engineering ecosystem evolves

The goal is not simply to publish documents.

The goal is to build a living engineering knowledge base.

⸻

🏗️ Text as an Engineering Element

Within Open Engineering, a text is more than a file.

A text can be an Open Engineering Element (OEE) with its own identity, metadata, relationships, evidence, provenance, and lifecycle.

A text may represent:

* a concept
* a definition
* an explanation
* a technical reference
* a design document
* an investigation
* a decision
* a specification
* a tutorial
* a course
* a case study
* a pattern
* a convention
* a research result
* a narrative
* a transcript
* a report

This makes texts composable building blocks rather than isolated documents.

⸻

✉️ Envelope · Letter · Library

Open Engineering uses a simple metaphor for knowledge:

✉️ Letter

The Letter is the content itself.

It contains the engineering knowledge — the explanation, investigation, specification, story, decision, or other useful material.

✉️ Envelope

The Envelope provides the context around the content.

It describes things such as identity, provenance, purpose, relationships, lifecycle, authorship, evidence, and metadata.

📚 Library

The Library is the continuously growing body of reusable knowledge.

Every useful Letter can become part of the Library, where it can be discovered, connected, transformed, and reused.

Envelope gives context. Letter contains knowledge. Library creates continuity.

⸻

🧠 Designed for Humans and AI

Open Engineering Texts treats engineering knowledge as a first-class input to both human and AI workflows.

Texts can be consumed by:

Engineers → AI Assistants → Investigations → Applications → Learning → New Knowledge

This enables workflows such as:

Source
  ↓
Text
  ↓
Structure
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Composition
  ↓
Engineering Product

The same knowledge can therefore support documentation, architecture, investigation, education, product development, and automation.

⸻

🔬 Evidence-Based Engineering Knowledge

Where appropriate, texts should retain their relationship to evidence.

A text may be derived from:

* source repositories
* specifications
* standards
* experiments
* investigations
* measurements
* conversations
* interviews
* source code
* diagrams
* external references
* other Open Engineering Elements

This creates a path from knowledge back to evidence.

That matters because useful engineering knowledge should not only answer:

What do we know?

It should also help answer:

Why do we believe it?

🌱 A Living Library

Open Engineering Texts is intended to grow continuously.

A text can evolve from:

Observation → Note → Text → Evidence → Knowledge → Pattern → Convention

And knowledge can flow in the other direction:

Library → Investigation → New Evidence → New Knowledge

This creates a feedback loop in which engineering knowledge becomes increasingly useful over time.
